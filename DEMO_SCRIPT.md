1. **0:00–0:30** Run the seed command and open the home page; explain that the
   banner means every record is synthetic DUMMY data.
2. **0:30–1:10** Open a deed's Verify link. Show **AUTHENTIC** and explain
   canonical JSON, SHA-256, Ed25519, and the chained ledger.
3. **1:10–1:40** Click **Tamper demo**. One dag digit changes and the result is
   **ALTERED** because the recomputed hash differs.
4. **1:40–2:15** Open Officer flags. Show SAME_SELLER_RESOLD, RAPID_TRANSFER,
   OVER_SOLD_AREA, and NO_MUTATION with Bengali and English reasons.
5. **2:15–3:00** Open Plot Safety Report for P-001. Walk through the simulated
   ownership timeline, mutation/case status, and plain-language risk summary.
